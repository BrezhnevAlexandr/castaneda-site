Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Castaneda Quotes" project ("the App") handles data. The App is a personal automation tool operated by its owner.

Data we process

  • Platform access tokens. To publish videos, the App stores OAuth access tokens for the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. Tokens are kept in a private, access-restricted location on the owner's machine and are never shared with third parties.
  • Generated content. Short videos and captions created by the App from public-domain-style quotations.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect personal information from viewers or from other platform users.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell or share any data.

Third-party APIs

The App interacts with TikTok and YouTube (Google) APIs solely to publish the owner's own content. Your use of those platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Data retention & deletion

Access tokens are held only as long as needed to operate the App and can be revoked by the owner at any time via the platform's account settings. To request deletion of any data, contact the address below.
